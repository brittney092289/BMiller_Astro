# Overview

The Astronomical League Observing Program Archive is a custom WordPress website that I designed, developed, and continue to maintain as a long-term record of my participation in Astronomical League observing programs.

The site organizes observation logs, astrophotography, equipment information, and observing program progress into a structured, searchable archive. It serves both as personal documentation of my observational work and as a platform for sharing that work with the broader amateur astronomy community.

## Motivation

As my observing activities expanded across multiple observing programs, I wanted a centralized system for documenting observations, imaging results, and equipment rather than relying on disconnected notes or folders.

My background in software engineering made developing a custom website a natural solution.

## Features

- Custom WordPress theme
- Responsive design
- Observation logs
- Astrophotography galleries
- Equipment documentation
- Observing program organization
- Long-term archive of astronomical observations

## Ongoing Development

This project is actively maintained as new observations, observing programs, and astrophotography are completed. The site continues to evolve through improvements to its design, organization, and functionality.
